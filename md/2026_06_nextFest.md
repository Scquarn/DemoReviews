# Next Fest June

All told her about the warmth of the first fire which was strong enough to overcome the cold. The day she finally approached the host grinned wide but there was no other greeting or even the slightest of nods to her. She was just one more soul adding to the crowd.
Whenever the warmth lessened the host brought fresh fuel and they threw it on the pyre together. It was named the feeding and not a single was ever missed. She thanked him and fully embraced his soothing gift that gave her a place to rest.
Time went on but the warmth stayed and she began to forget the cold that had always been there. Stoking the fire became routine. It had to be done.
No matter how many they became, the warmth spread to all. With the cold slowly weakening the host’s grin widened and it never wavered. Not even when he brought the child to throw on the pyre. It cried and begged but on the pyre it went all the same and nobody questioned it. The fire needed fuel and their routine was to stoke the flames with whatever the host brought, this was simply how things went. Fuel went on the pyre and they did it together for not a single feeding could be missed.
The flames were always hungry and the host brought more innocents to burn. Despite that she still held out her hands towards the warmth that came from the smouldering remains on the pyre irate. It was unjust and wrong but she didn’t want to feel the cold again. The fire had to survive.
Time went on and the bone pile grew. Became as tall as a mountain, fell over, rose again, spread far and further. Warmth everywhere, warmth for all. What once was frozen land became part of the pyre and the warmth increased with every feeding. Became hot, turned scorching.
For a long time she never had doubts but then the feeding was interrupted by pure chance. Fuel brought to burn had fallen off the pyre. It was only for a moment before it was pushed back in the flames, but it was enough. For that split second the warmth faltered, the temperature lessened for fraction. A change so small, yet it reminded her of the past and of the long forgotten cold. She remembered how it felt not to burn, how it was to breathe fresh air, how ice had sounded in the wind, how snow had fallen from the hollow grey sky and she realised that she longed for it. She wanted to feel the touch of frost on her skin once more.
It made her turn away from the heat, made her search for the cold eternal but no matter where she went the pyre had already spread. The land was taken by ash and the air filled with smoke.
Desperate she turned back and looked for the host, to ask him if there was still a place where the cold had remained but she could not find him. The pyre burned alone and the next feeding came but he wasn’t there to bring new fuel. The people turned to her and asked what to do but she had no answer either.
Again she felt the warmth wavering and now the people felt it too but for them it didn’t create longing but terror. They had heard whispers of the freezing, the world savage. The fear connected them all together like invisible threads of a giant web and that single pull on it was enough to alert all of them. A new feeding began but this time without the host’s guidance and whatever they could find was thrown on the pyre, including themselves. Everything to the flames in hope it wouldn’t fade but nothing helped.
Scorching turned to heat, turned to warmth and finally the fire died and left behind neither warmth nor cold but the empty ever dark.
In the last shimmers of the fire she saw the host laying at a far away arm of the pyre, the everlasting grin still on his lips. He was dead and with the pyre reduced to it’s last glimmers she made a choice. Taking one of the sparks in her hands she understood the reason for why it had burned and with the shadows approaching only two things remained: Endless screams of those lost in the dark and a wide grin on lips illuminated by the last light.

## Demos

It has come: Next Fest. No doubt about it as this blog has reappeared before you. This assembly of words and thoughts mad. Like a comet it graces your orbit and may it only mirror Halley: Rare but ever reoccurring. I do consider that it’s late, but it does exist and it’s here. Enjoy.

### 1

Our first climb in this Next Fest will be with [Ascenders: Beyond the Peak](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4267860/Ascenders_Beyond_the_Peak/). Here we guide a team of three climbers in a turn-based exploration roguelite with survival elements sprinkled in. On paper it sounds interesting and so do the screenshots on the steam page but I found it rather meh.  
Don't misunderstand, the mechanics are well done. You are climbing with a team of three, where each member has a different skillset that helps survive and reminds of other similar team roguelites like darkest dungeon. Each turn you have to decide between different actions like collecting berries for food, climbing the wall, evade rocks falling from above and other similar stuff. Between each climbing level you arrive at camp where you can level and upgrade your team, eat food and upgrade the camp. Found items have to be puzzled in the backpack Tetris style. On top of all that there are also day and night mechanics, different terrains, and lots more.
There is a lot of stuff in here but I don't vibe with it. Nothing is out of place but also nothing is in the right place to make me interested to continue the climb. Demo is still on steam, better take a look yourself for this one, for me it's a no.

### 4

Talking about flashgames, let's take a look at [GemCraft - Legacy Collection](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4207990/GemCraft__Legacy_Collection/). From a shitty flash like to an actual former flashgame. Seems like there are a few of them coming to steam recently? Saw happy wheels too.
Anyways, this is GemCraft. One of the original baller Tower Defenses. Have you played this before, then you already know this is good but for all the zoomers that don't know the OG let's explain:
This is the classic TD experience. Each level has a set path the monsters walk to your base and you have to create towers to defend against them but the unique spin in this game is that towers are really cheap and the real focus are the gems that you place inside them. Each gem can be upgraded by combining two of the same tier. There are about eight total different gems, from some that deal high damage, have high range, poison the enemy, slow, have splash damage and some others. The fun part is that any type of gem can be combined together with others, they just must have the same tier. This means you can create some cool combinations like a slowing splash, a high range damage gem, whatever you can come up with.
The game is a damn classic and this combines all three, GemCraft 1, Gemcraft 0 and GemCraft Labyrinth into a single Collection. Go play the demo if you have never played one of these, they are a lot of fun. Keeping an eye on this one, maybe getting it if it's cheap.

### 5

From strategic tower placement to actual strategy in [ZeroSpace](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1605850/ZeroSpace/), a new RTS game.
I open the game and see there are three modes: campaign, galactic war and versus. Let's check out the campaign for now. A cutscene starts and story is really basic: Humans use Flux for FTL, humans want more flux, the aliens have the flux, you know what happens next.
On the first mission we crash on an alien planet with our small task force and have to fight along a few waypoints to get more units and to defend the aliens who are a bit plant like at times. During this I notice that the dialogue in this game has some fun lines so I'm going to include some in this like this one, "We are up shits creek without a paddle".
Finish the mission to find some weird super alien that we collect and get promoted for. No clue what it is, highly classified you know the drill. Story is kinda basic tbh but it's fun.
Actual gameplay feels nice to. Controls are smooth and the classic RTS mechanics are all here, so it checks all the boxes for me. Between the missions you can walk around the ship and talk to the other members in your crew and the dialogue with them is well made too. "A cesspool of incompetence and neopetism but we are all swimming in it together."
There is still a bit of scuff, like cutscenes getting cut off or the UI being a bit rough at places but overall it's a decently made game. I didn't try the other modes much because I tired galactic war but didn't understand how the mode worked as the tutorial didn't explain anything at all. The chat was spammed in chinese, so there's that.

### 8

Ahoi matey and welcome to the [Corsair Cove](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3858730/Corsair_Cove_Demo/). A pirate city builder. The loading screen seems infinite but after around five minutes the game starts. Our crew loses their ships and we have to start over by building a new pirate settlement on a small island. The graphics are awesome, rich blue water, lush grenery, a giant skull shaped mountain in the center of the island. In short, it looks damn beautiful.
It's a fun city builder as everything feels very organic. You place down buildings and connect them together with paths, seems normal at first but then you realize that most of the building happens vertically and the paths turn into wooden bridges, ladders and suspension bridges. The further you build upwards on the island the more paths get created, almost like inside an anthill. You have to satisfy the classic needs like food, stone, wood, etc but there are also more unique mechanics. If people get to unhappy they will mutaneer and if it gets too low the game is lost.
There are also open sea battles that get fought with the ships you build. The battles are turn based and similar to classic roguelike card games, but it seems here we get set decks for them depending on the ship you have built.
Overall the game is great, a city builder that convinces for a change. Good game.

### 11

Let's visit space in [Hull Rupture](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4809240/Hull_Rupture_Demo/). At first i thought this was a game like FTL: Faster than light but this is actually a tower defense and it's made by the makers of despot's game which was already quite interesting.
In this game we build a spaceship, manage different resources like people in your crew, energy resources, etc and we build towers on the outside of the ship to defend against incoming waves. It's a bit of a mix between tower defense, city builder and roguelte. We also get different artifacts and random shops after each wave.
It's really fun and trying to get the ship up and running to fit higher tier rooms and towers is a lot of fun. I'll wishlist this one as the demo makes me want to keep playing for another round even after I have already done quite a few.
